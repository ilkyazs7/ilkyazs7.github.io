- type: text
  text: The first 4 or 5 mm or so is simply due to the initially slack connection between the bottom split ring and the M6 thumbscrew in the bottom clevis connection being taken up. Then we have a high initial slope (i.e., high spring stiffness) as the compressed spring coils become progressively separated. After this, we get an “elbow” like transition to the final lower slope (i.e., lower stiffness) spring response region. This is a common finding for such tightly wound springs.
